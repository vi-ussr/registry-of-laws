## Article 1. Requests for copies
{: #article-1}

A request for an archival copy shall identify the record by title, date or register number.

1. The archive shall record the request in its correspondence book.
2. An incomplete reference may be returned with a request for further particulars.

## Article 2. Certification
{: #article-2}

The authorised clerk shall compare the copy with the preserved record and certify its accuracy by signature.

## Article 3. Missing records
{: #article-3}

Where the requested record cannot be located, the archive shall issue a written notice identifying the material searched.
