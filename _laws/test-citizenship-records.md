## Article 1. Applications
{: #article-1}

Applications for a citizenship certificate shall be submitted to the district office responsible for civil records.

1. The applicant shall state their full name, place of birth and current residence.
2. Supporting records shall be listed in an inventory attached to the application.

## Article 2. Examination of records
{: #article-2}

The office shall compare the application with the civil register and record any correction before issuing a certificate.

## Article 3. Replacement copies
{: #article-3}

A replacement shall be marked as a duplicate and shall retain the number of the original certificate.
