## Article 1. Maintenance of the register
{: #article-1}

Every local Soviet shall maintain a register of decisions issued within its jurisdiction.

1. Each decision shall receive a consecutive number and a date of publication.
2. The secretary shall enter the title and issuing authority without abbreviation.

## Article 2. Inspection and extracts
{: #article-2}

The register shall be available for inspection during the ordinary hours of the office. Copies shall bear the number and date of the original decision.

## Article 3. Custody of records
{: #article-3}

Completed volumes shall be transferred to the district archive with an inventory of their contents.
