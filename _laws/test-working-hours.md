## Article 1. Office schedules
{: #article-1}

Each administrative office shall display its public opening hours at the entrance and maintain a duty roster.

1. The roster shall identify the officer responsible for receiving visitors.
2. Changes shall be entered before the beginning of the affected duty period.

## Article 2. Continuity of service
{: #article-2}

The head of the office shall arrange substitute duty where the assigned officer is absent.

## Article 3. Commencement
{: #article-3}

This resolution takes effect on 1 December 2026. Existing schedules shall remain in use until that date.
