## Article 1. Scope of registration
{: #article-1}

State enterprises engaged in production, transport or distribution shall be entered in the economic register.

1. The entry shall state the official name, location and responsible ministry.
2. A separate entry shall be maintained for each enterprise.

## Article 2. Changes to an entry
{: #article-2}

The director shall notify the registering office within seven days of a change of name or address. The previous entry shall remain available in the archive.

## Article 4. Annual verification
{: #article-4}

The registering office shall verify each entry annually and record the date of verification.
