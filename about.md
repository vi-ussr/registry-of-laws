The Register of Laws brings together laws, decrees, resolutions and instructions in one searchable publication. Each document records its number, issuing authority, publication date and effective date.

## Finding a document

Use the [chronological register]({{ '/' | relative_url }}) to browse publications, the [subject index]({{ '/subjects/' | relative_url }}) to browse topics, or the [publication archive]({{ '/bulletins/' | relative_url }}) to consult an issue.

Search can narrow results by title or article text, document type, authority, legal status and publication date.

## Texts and dates

The register date is **{{ site.register_date }}**. A document's main page shows the latest text effective on that date. Historical and future texts remain available under History. Status labels distinguish texts not yet effective, instruments in force, and repealed instruments.

Publication, commencement and repeal are distinct dates. A preserved text remains available after repeal. Relationships between instruments help readers follow references; they do not change the published wording automatically.
